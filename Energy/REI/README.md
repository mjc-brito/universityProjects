# Smart Electric Grids

This repository contains the final project files for the course in Smart Electric Grids, part of the Master's program in Electrical and Computer Engineering.

## Repository Contents

### Final Project: Construction and Simulation of an Electric Grid

The final project focuses on the modeling and simulation of a section of the Portuguese electric grid, aiming to analyze power flow, contingencies, and fault scenarios, including short circuits. Key aspects of the project include:

1. **Modeling**:
   - Development of a detailed model representing a portion of the Portuguese National Transmission Network (RNT) and Distribution Network (RND), including substations and associated components.
   - Use of realistic data sourced from REN and E-REDES to ensure accurate modeling of lines, transformers, generators, and buses.
   - Integration of equivalent generators to represent parts of the grid outside the selected section.

2. **Validation**:
   - Power flow analysis under normal operating conditions to ensure voltage levels and line loadings meet established standards.
   - Contingency analysis ("N-1" scenarios) to evaluate the grid's robustness under element failures, with proposed improvements such as new lines and capacitor banks to enhance reliability.
   - Short-circuit analysis to identify the most critical fault scenarios and their impact on the system.

3. **Software Tools**:
   - The entire project was conducted using the PSSE simulation software, allowing for the creation, validation, and analysis of the grid model.

4. **Key Findings**:
   - Voltage levels in the transmission and distribution networks were maintained within acceptable limits under normal operation, with minor exceptions in the 60 kV buses.
   - Contingency scenarios highlighted critical vulnerabilities, which were addressed through model modifications, including additional lines and optimized transformer ratios.
   - Short-circuit analysis revealed the most critical fault scenarios, with recommendations provided for system protection.

The report includes all results, visualizations, and detailed insights into the model's performance under various scenarios.
